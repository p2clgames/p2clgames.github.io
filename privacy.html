Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the mobile games (hereinafter the “Application”) provided free of charge by P2CLGames (hereinafter the “Company” or “P2CL”).

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages

The Application does not collect location information from your mobile device.

The Application does not use artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or to provide its features.

P2CL may use the information you provide to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience while using the Application, P2CL may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information. The information that P2CL requests will be retained and used as described in this privacy policy.

  • Google Play Services
  • Unity

P2CL may disclose information you provide and information collected automatically:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with our trusted service providers who work on our behalf, do not use the information we disclose to them independently, and have agreed to follow the rules set out in this privacy policy.

Opt-Out Rights

You can easily stop all collection of information by uninstalling the app. Use the standard uninstall process provided by your mobile device or the app marketplace/network.

Data Retention Policy

The Company retains the data you provide for as long as you use the app and for a reasonable time thereafter. If you would like the data you provided through the app to be deleted, please contact us at p2clgames@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

P2CL does not use this Application to collect data from, or market to, children under the age of 13.

This Application is not directed at anyone under the age of 13. P2CL does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it from our servers immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and you know that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at p2clgames@gmail.com so that we can take the necessary action.

Security

P2CL is committed to protecting your information. P2CL maintains physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to protect the information it processes and stores.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. P2CL will notify you of changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page. Please review this privacy policy regularly for any changes. Continued use of the service is deemed acceptance of all changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of March 5, 2026.

プライバシーポリシー

本プライバシーポリシーは、P2CLGames（以下「当社」または「P2CL」）が無料で提供するモバイルゲーム（以下「本アプリ」）に適用されます。

情報の収集と利用

本アプリは、ユーザーが本アプリをダウンロードして利用する際に情報を収集します。収集される情報には、次の内容が含まれる場合があります。

  • ユーザーの端末のインターネットプロトコルアドレス（例：IPアドレス）
  • ユーザーが訪れた本アプリのページ、訪問日時、そのページで過ごした時間
  • 本アプリで過ごした時間
  • ユーザーのモバイル端末で使用しているオペレーティングシステム

本アプリは、ユーザーのモバイル端末の位置情報を収集しません。

本アプリは、ユーザーのデータの処理や機能の提供に人工知能（AI）技術を使用しません。

P2CLは、重要なお知らせ、必要な通知、マーケティングプロモーションをお届けするため、ユーザーから提供された情報を利用して随時ユーザーに連絡することがあります。

本アプリをより快適にご利用いただくため、P2CLはユーザーに特定の個人識別情報の提供をお願いすることがあります。P2CLが求めた情報は保管され、本プライバシーポリシーに記載のとおりに使用されます。

本アプリは、データの取り扱いについて独自のプライバシーポリシーを持つ第三者サービスを利用しています。本アプリが利用する第三者サービス提供者のプライバシーポリシーへのリンクは次のとおりです。

  • Unity
